Built for the people air hits hardest

Smoke and smog aren't equal-opportunity. These groups get protective advice sooner.

  • Asthma & COPD

    Fine-particle smoke can trigger attacks well before the air looks "bad".

  • Heart conditions

    Pollution stresses the cardiovascular system, raising real risk.

  • Children

    Smaller, developing lungs take in more air per kilo of body weight.

  • Pregnancy

    Exposure is linked to outcomes for both parent and baby.

  • Older adults

    Reduced lung reserve makes poor air more dangerous.

  • Outdoor workers

    Hours of exertion outside multiply every exposure.
